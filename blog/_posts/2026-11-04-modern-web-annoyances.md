Look, I get it...

these things all come from real world problems.

I was working on my first scraper when I drafted this, and realized just how incredible it is that we even still have visibility into what makes the internet the Internet. I’m actually shocked that browsers are still the main way to access the internet, and that they still allow us to read the source code and data.

I also realize that I’ve made basically my entire career off of working against that. Apps and mobile devices are the opaque appliances of the internet. There are no developer tools for corporate apps, nor a way to view the source code running or the data being transmitted (at least, not nearly as easily).

Viewed through that lens, it’s understandable that companies with profit motive would want to push us from an open Internet experience to a closed walled garden in their app. That doesn't mean this isn't a degraded experience that seems built by people out of touch with their users.

It’s better in the app! Better for their bottom line.
