--- layout: null title: Books --- {%- comment -%} Where the authors of the books on my Goodreads shelves were born, on a globe. The data is in _data/: books.yml from the Goodreads export (make books-import), authors.yml from Wikidata (make books-authors), each author filed under a country by make books-geo, and the former state they were born in, where there is one, by make books-history. The borders are today's. An author born in a state that no longer exists is shown where that place is now, and the list says what it was then. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign placed = site.data.authors | where_exp: "author", "author.country" -%} {%- assign unplaced = site.data.authors | where_exp: "author", "author.country == nil" | sort: "name" -%} {%- assign shelves = "read,currently-reading,to-read" | split: "," -%} {%- comment -%} The tally at the top: how many of the 195 countries in states.yml have an author on the read shelf, and how many on the reading shelf. Legend 1 authors none

Each country is coloured by how many authors were born there, and each dot is a birthplace. Drag to turn the globe, scroll to move closer, and click a country or a dot to look closely at it and find it in the list. Escape, or a click on the ocean, steps back.

{%- comment -%} The same authors as a list, written by the server, so it reads with no script at all. Countries start shut; choosing one on the globe opens it. Each book carries its shelf as a class, so the script can hide the ones on other shelves than the one shown, and then the authors and countries left empty. On a wide screen the whole list starts folded away to the right, with a button to bring it back (outline-fold.js, shared with travel). {%- endcomment -%}
