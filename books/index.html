--- layout: null title: Books --- {%- comment -%} Where the authors of the books on my Goodreads shelves were born, on a globe. The data is in _data/: books.yml from the Goodreads export (make books-import), authors.yml from Wikidata (make books-authors), each author filed under a country by make books-geo, and the former state they were born in, where there is one, by make books-history. The borders are today's. An author born in a state that no longer exists is shown where that place is now, and the list says what it was then. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign placed = site.data.authors | where_exp: "author", "author.country" -%} {%- assign unplaced = site.data.authors | where_exp: "author", "author.country == nil" | sort: "name" -%} {%- assign shelves = "read,currently-reading,to-read" | split: "," -%} {%- comment -%} The tally at the top: how many of the 195 countries in states.yml have an author on the read shelf, and how many on the reading shelf. An author counts under the state their birthplace is in today (state: in authors.yml), however the borders ran when they were born. {%- endcomment -%} {%- for shelf in shelves limit: 2 -%} {%- assign shelf_authors = site.data.books | where: "shelf", shelf | map: "author" | uniq -%} {%- assign shelf_states = "" -%} {%- for author in placed -%} {%- if author.state and shelf_authors contains author.name -%} {%- assign shelf_states = shelf_states | append: author.state | append: "," -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- assign shelf_state_count = shelf_states | split: "," | uniq | size -%} {%- if shelf == "read" -%}{%- assign read_states = shelf_state_count -%}{%- else -%}{%- assign reading_states = shelf_state_count -%}{%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%} Books — Andrew McKnight {%- comment -%} The build time on our own files makes a browser fetch them again after every build, as travel's page does. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign build = site.time | date: '%s' %}

Each country is coloured by how many authors were born there, and each dot is a birthplace. Drag to turn the globe, scroll to move closer, and click a country or a dot to look closely at it and find it in the list. Escape, or a click on the ocean, steps back.
