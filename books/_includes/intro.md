I like to read. Fiction and nonfiction, prose and poetry. Some time ago I formulated a goal to read something published by an author from every country, to pull from a diverse set of life experiences and worldviews. This is a way to visualize my progress on this quest.

It sounded simple when I came up with it, but the devil is in the details. What about countries that no longer exist as they did when the author lived there? What about places that aren't/weren't "countries" at all, but rather territories, colonies? What if an author was born in one place, but moved to another before/after publishing the work that I read? What about expansive countries that really contain a multitude of subcultures, can they really be represented by a single person? What about societies with histories of oppression, colonialization, or multiple warring factions? What about those that experienced successive waves of immigration over time? What if no author is known for a given work?

For now, and for simplicity's sake, I'll simply record the city of birth of the author, and project it onto whatever present-day country or territory in which it resides. In the future I'd like to add a timeline slider that changes which borders are drawn on the globe.

The question of whether to assign them to their country of origin or country of residence while writing the given work is tougher. On one hand, where you come from inexorably colors your life story, which by the way doesn't just start with you, but with your parents, their parents, and so on. But, where your formative experiences occur matters too, as well as pivotal moments like uprooting your life and moving to a very different, faraway place. I could represent all of these by drawing not just a dot for an author, but a path.

I plan to evolve this project to address these questions over time, and as I read. I'm sure I'll come up with new questions, too.
